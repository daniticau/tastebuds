What it keeps, and what it does not

Tastebuds cannot say who you are. That is by design.

  • It keeps your food preferences and your opinions on places, under a random token.
  • It never keeps your name, your number, your messages, or your location history.
  • Friend and circle signals show counts only, never who said what.
  • Ask your assistant what it remembers about your taste. Ask it to forget, and it deletes the profile.
