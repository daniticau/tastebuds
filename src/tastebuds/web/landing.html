What happens next

  1. Your assistant asks one question: a couple of spots you love, and anything you do not eat.
  2. Ask it where to eat. The picks fit your taste from the first day.
  3. Tell it how the meal went, in your own words. The next pick gets better, for you and for everyone.

What it keeps, and what it does not

  • It keeps your food preferences and your opinions on places, under a random token.
  • It never keeps your name, your number, your messages, or your location history.
  • Your assistant can link you with the friends you message most, so their taste counts more. It sends a closeness level from 1 to 3, never names, numbers, or messages.
  • Friend and circle signals show counts only, never who said what.
  • Ask your assistant what it remembers about your taste. Ask it to forget, and it deletes the profile.
